The Vision

Bridging blockchain and humanitarian impact

Gracia Token exists to close the distance between financial innovation and human restoration. Every transaction contributes to a treasury with a stated purpose, and every allocation is recorded on a public ledger that anyone can read. Transparency is not a promise here — it is the mechanism.

We hold ourselves to the standards of the institutions we admire: accountability in how funds are held, clarity in how they are spent, and compassion in who they reach. Grace is the intent. Gain is the means. Both are measured.
